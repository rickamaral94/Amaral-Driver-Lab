About confidence

corroborated means the same driver pair was submitted independently at least 2 times on the same GPU. single submission means one person ran it once. Nothing here is cryptographically verified: any signature the app could produce, anyone who unpacked the APK could produce too, so this project does not claim verification it cannot deliver.
